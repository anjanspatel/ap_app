Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026  ·  Jurisdiction: Alberta, Canada

Who we are

AP Workspace is operated by Anjan Patel, an individual based in Alberta, Canada. This policy describes how we handle personal information collected through app.anjanpatel.ca.

Calculator inputs stay local until you save

The Flange, Torque, and Safety Block calculators run entirely in your browser. Values you type in — bore size, bolt grade, BHN, preload, and so on — are not transmitted anywhere just by entering them; they exist only in that page's memory. If you use the "Save to Dashboard" action, that specific lookup (its inputs, computed result, and an optional label) is sent to your authenticated account on our database provider, as described below. Nothing is saved or transmitted unless you take that action, and closing the tab without saving discards it completely.

What we collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Email address — provided when you create an account or sign in via Supabase Auth.
  • Saved lookups — the inputs and outputs you explicitly choose to save using the Save button inside a calculator (tool name, parameters, optional label, result summary, timestamp).
  • Session data — a session token stored in a browser cookie to keep you signed in. This cookie is strictly necessary and expires with your session or on sign-out.

We do not collect browsing history, device fingerprints, or any data beyond the above.

How we use it

  • Authenticating your identity and maintaining your session.
  • Storing and displaying your saved calculator results on your dashboard.
  • No marketing, profiling, or automated decision-making.

Third-party services

We use Supabase (supabase.com) for authentication and database storage. Your email address and saved lookup data are stored on Supabase infrastructure. Supabase is SOC 2 Type II certified. Review their privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy.

We use Plausible Analytics (plausible.io) for aggregate page-view statistics on public pages. Plausible is cookieless and collects no personal data — no IP addresses, no device fingerprints, no cross-site tracking. Data is not shared with third parties. Review their data policy at plausible.io/privacy.

No other third-party services receive your personal data. We do not use advertising networks, social tracking pixels, or analytics services that share data with third parties.

Cookies

We use one cookie: a session token set by Supabase Auth when you sign in. It is strictly necessary for the service to function — without it you cannot remain authenticated. We do not use tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies.

Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual saved lookups from your dashboard at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us using the address below.

Your rights (PIPEDA / Alberta PIPA)

Under Canadian federal and Alberta provincial privacy legislation you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you.
  • Request a copy of your data.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted via TLS. Data at rest is encrypted by Supabase. Access to your data is enforced by row-level security policies — your saved lookups are only accessible by your authenticated account. The site is served exclusively over HTTPS.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

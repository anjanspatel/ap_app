What these tools are

AP Workspace's calculators — Flange Slide Rule, Bolt Torque Calculator, and Safety Block Calculator — are reference aids that apply published formulas and standard tables to the numbers you enter. They are built for speed in the field, not for certification.

What they are not

AP Workspace outputs are generated for reference and preliminary analysis only. They do not constitute certified engineering advice, a stamped engineering sign-off, or a substitute for review by a qualified engineer. This applies to every output the tools produce, including but not limited to:

  • Flange dimensions, bolt circles, bore sizes, and ring gasket designations
  • Bolt torque values, nut factors, and crossbolt sequence guidance
  • Safe working load (SWL) and Von Mises stress estimates
  • Any pressure rating, pressure limit, or material property referenced by the tools

All torque values, pressure limits, and flange specifications must be independently verified by a licensed Professional Engineer (P.E. / P.Eng.) prior to field execution. Do not use any output as the sole basis for a safety-critical decision, equipment selection, installation, or recertification.

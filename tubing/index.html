Calculation Steps
Engineering estimate, not a certified result. This uses a common field approximation (UTS ≈ 500 × BHN) and applies the 0.577 shear/tensile ratio to that estimated UTS rather than to a measured yield strength — a simplification, not a cited calculation procedure from ASTM/API/ASME. Treat this figure as a starting reference only; a Professional Engineer must independently verify safe working load before field use.

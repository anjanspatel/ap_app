Frequently Asked

K depends on lubrication: Dry (no lube) K≈0.20, Lightly oiled K≈0.15, Heavy grease K≈0.13, MoS₂ paste K≈0.12, PTFE tape K≈0.10, Nickel anti-seize K≈0.13. These are typical values — always verify with your lubricant supplier's datasheet. Using the wrong K can result in under- or over-torqued joints, risking leak or yielding.
A single-pass torque produces uneven clamp load distribution — bolts tightened early relax as adjacent bolts are torqued. The 3-pass crossbolt sequence (30% → 70% → 100%) per ASME PCC-1 ensures even load distribution around the flange. Crossing to the bolt ~180° opposite prevents the flange from tilting and distorting the gasket seating surface.
ASME PCC-1 and most API 6A JIPs recommend 60–70% of proof load for standard service. Sour/H₂S service per NACE MR0175 requires A193 B7M (lower hardness) and limits stress to avoid sulphide stress cracking — always check the project-specific bolting specification. Exceeding 80% proof load approaches yield and risks thread galling on re-use.
A193 B7 is standard chrome-moly alloy with high strength (Sy=105 ksi for ≤1" dia). B7M is a modified grade with hardness limited to 235 HBW maximum for sour-service compatibility per NACE MR0175. B7M has lower proof strength (~80–95 ksi) so torque values are lower. L7 and L7M are similar low-temperature variants used below −45°C.
Formula — T = K × D × F
T = torque, K = nut factor, D = nominal bolt diameter (in), F = clamp load (lb). Proof load = tensile stress area × proof strength (ASME PCC-1 Appendix K).
B7 Proof Strength
≤ 1" dia: 105,000 psi  |  1-1/8" – 1-1/2": 95,000 psi  |  > 1-1/2": 75,000 psi. Per ASTM A193 / API 20E.
Nut Factor (K) guidance
Dry: 0.20 · Oil: 0.15 · Grease: 0.13 · MoS₂: 0.12 · Ni anti-seize: 0.13 · PTFE: 0.10. Always verify K with lube supplier data sheet.
